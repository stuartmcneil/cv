Overview

Three decades spanning studio practice and higher education — from BAFTA-winning interactive media for the BBC, BT and the European Commission, to writing degree programmes and examining them across Europe, North Africa, the Gulf and South-East Asia; a founding member and vice chair of the arts charity Pedestrian; and, having led a department where robotics, cyber security and interaction design sit side by side, now leading Software Engineering and sitting on the management team for the Cardiff School of Technologies.

Stuart McNeil is Head of Department for Software Engineering at Cardiff Metropolitan University, supporting a team of academics whose disciplines run from electronics engineering and robotics to human–computer interaction, cyber security and information systems, and leading the pedagogic development of the department's undergraduate and postgraduate awards. His doctorate, begun in 2000, is due for submission in September 2026.

He has taught on computing and design-led modules for over twenty years, focused on interaction design and software engineering management, and has led the development of numerous programmes at both undergraduate and postgraduate level. He has held External Examiner and Moderator roles at universities in the UK, Oman, Egypt and Spain, giving him a global perspective on computing and design education.

Before academia — and alongside it, through his own studio Deft Design — he worked in the interactive media sector, winning a BAFTA for Best New Media and developing creative solutions for international organisations and leading UK companies including the European Commission, the Foreign Office, the Intellectual Property Office, BT and the BBC. That real-world experience ignited a drive to attain the highest standards in design practice. He was also a founding member and vice chair of the arts charity Pedestrian, serving on its board across thirteen years.
