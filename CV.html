Statement about Stuart McNeil:

Stuart McNeil is a Senior Lecturer for the Cardiff Metropolitan University, within the Department of Applied Computing and Engineering in the Cardiff School of Technologies where he is a Senior Lecturer for the Computing for Interaction – BSc (Hons) Degree, Computing with Creative Design – BSc (Hons) Degree, BSc Software Engineering , BSc Computing and BSc Business Information Systems Undergraduate programmes. Additionally, Stuart is the Module Leader for MSc and BSc Technology Project Management. Stuart is a Member of the British Computer Society, Human Computer Interface group; Member of the Association for Computing Machinery – Special Interest group and a Fellow of the Higher Education Academy (FHEA) Stuart has previously worked within the interactive media sector for numerous years and gained awards which included a BAFTA. Working with some international organisations and some of the UKs leading companies, such as: The European Commission; The Foreign Office; The Patent Office (Intellectual Property Office UK); Central Office of Information; GEC Marconi; General Electric (GE); Rockwool; Amec; Elf Oil; British Telecom (BT); Docklands Light Railway (DLR); East of Scotland Water; Scottish Power; Dwr Cymru Welsh Water (DCWW); South Wales Electricity Board (SWALEC); South West Electricity Board (SWEB); Hyder; Highways Agency; Gwent Police; The Millennium Commission. Stuart’s industrial experience focused on developing creative solutions to the interactive problems of their trade, this real-world experience ignited a drive to attain the highest of standards in design practice.Stuart was the Managing Director of Deft Design for 17 years , this experience proved foundational in the understandings of directing a company and managing creative and highly skilled projects and teams. Additionally, Stuart was a founding charity director and trustee of ‘Pedestrian Arts’ Charitable Non-Profit National Arts Company for 13 years. Judicially, Stuart reviews papers for and is a member of the British Computer Society, Human Computer Interface group and has been a Judge for BAFTA, The Golden Pixel Awards and the Celtic Film Festival . Additionally, Stuart has held numerous External Examiner and Moderator roles at many universities around the world, these have seen him attain a global perspective into Computing and Design.

The ‘This working stuff is really cool’ points of my career

I have been very fortunate to have some really fantastic experiences throughout my career some memorable moments would have to include: A helicopter shoot at 250 ft above ‘Tower Bridge’ with the sun flaring over the spires of London’s skyline; Having the corporate marketing tech team (London) at BT stood around my work and ask “How did you do that?”; Winning a BAFTA for ‘best new media’, when I had no idea I was even entered; Being involved with the excitement and passion of students researching and developing creative and inspirational work; Onstage at Hackney Marshes with Musicians: Ice-T, Mark-B & Blade, Biz Markie and Rodney-P; Watching a young child who had been expelled from school and excluded for the best part of his formative years, get his first qualification without realising..
